# Marmaradar

**Marmaradar** helps drivers in the Marmara region stay aware of fixed speed cameras (EDS) and average-speed corridors — starting with Bursa.

Open the map, start a drive, and get clear warnings before you reach a camera or enter a corridor. Alerts keep working in the background so you can focus on the road.

## What it does

- **Live map** — see your position, nearby cameras, and corridor stretches on the map
- **Speed camera alerts** — get notified as you approach a fixed camera, with distance and speed limit
- **Average-speed corridors** — track your average speed vs the limit while you’re inside a corridor
- **Background warnings** — alerts continue with the screen off or the app in the background
- **Destination search** — search where you’re going and follow a driving route with distance and ETA
- **Automatic tracking** — optionally start tracking when driving is detected

## Who it’s for

Drivers who regularly travel in and around **Bursa** and the wider **Marmara** region and want a simple, on-the-road alert companion — not a full navigation replacement.

## Platforms

Marmaradar is built for **Android** and **iOS**.

> App Store / Play Store listings are not published yet. Builds are currently shared privately.

## How to use it

1. Install the app and allow **location** (and **notifications**). For alerts while the phone is locked, choose **always** location access and turn off battery restrictions for Marmaradar where your phone asks.
2. Open the map and wait for GPS to lock onto your position.
3. Tap **Sürüşe Başla** to start tracking, or turn on **Otomatik** so tracking can begin when you start driving.
4. Optionally search a destination (**Nereye?**) and follow the route on the map.
5. Watch for camera and corridor alerts; tap a camera marker for more detail.
6. Tap **Sürüşü Bitir** when you’re done.

## Privacy

Marmaradar needs your location to warn you about cameras and corridors nearby. Location is used for driving alerts and map tracking — not for social features or advertising.
